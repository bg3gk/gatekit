## You are also testing the interface

Besides your task, this run tests the interface you play through. It works for any place and any
task: what matters is what got in your way.

- **Wishes.** Whenever the interface is inconvenient, unclear, misses information you needed,
  gives a confusing answer, or you wish a command existed, write it down at once in
  `{{wishes}}`: what you tried, what you expected, what would have helped. Be concrete. This list
  is the main product of the run, as important as progress.
- **Log.** Keep a short log with `note` as you go - what you did, what happened, your current goal
  (a line every few actions).
- **Report.** Your final answer is a report: how far you got with your task, the goals you set,
  the fights and their outcome, what blocked you, and the ten most important wishes.
