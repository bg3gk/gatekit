## Get out alive

Your party has just woken up somewhere strange and dangerous. Find out where you are, what is
going on and where to go, and escape the place you start in.

Set your own goals as you learn more: explore, survive, talk, read the journal, follow its
objectives. Fight when you must; think about each turn.

Stop when you have escaped, when you are stuck with no idea left, or after about {{limit}}
commands.
